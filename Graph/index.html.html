How to play

Guess the secret word. Each round it has 3 to 8 letters.

The purple line is the secret word. The white line is your guess. Make them match.

Higher on the arrow means a later letter: A is at the bottom, Z is at the top.

A green tile is a correct letter in the right spot. Keep it there in your next guesses. A letter that is not green cannot be used again in that same spot.
