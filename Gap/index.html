How to play

Guess the hidden word. Each tile's color shows how far your letter is from the right letter in that spot, counting along the alphabet. The distance can be forward or backward.

1 to 5 away6 to 10 away11 to 15 away16 to 20 away21 to 25 awayCorrect letter

Example: M is the 13th letter. An orange M means the right letter is 6 to 10 places away: S to W going forward, or C to G going backward.
