Word Shift

Each letter tells you which way to move in the alphabet.

Blue ←go backward in the alphabet
Greenright spot
Purple →go forward in the alphabet

How to play

Guess the secret word. After each guess, every letter gets a color that tells you how it compares with the secret word.

Green: right spot. Blue ←: the secret letter at that spot comes earlier in the alphabet. Purple →: the secret letter at that spot comes later.

Blue and purple only show a direction. They do not tell you whether the letter is in the word.

Every new guess must also fit all the colors so far.
